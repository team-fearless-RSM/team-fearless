06 — Engineering Journey

Build it. Break it. Improve it.

Robotics is not one big moment. It is a loop of ideas, prototypes, failures, fixes and better decisions.

01 / IDEA
💡

Imagine

Understand the challenge, explore possibilities and turn a problem into a robot idea.

02 / DESIGN
📐

Design

Sketch mechanisms, think through constraints and choose a design worth building.

03 / BUILD
🔧

Build

Fabricate, assemble, wire and bring the first prototype to life.

04 / TEST
🧪

Test

Run the robot, find what fails and collect the feedback the field gives us.

05 / IMPROVE
⚡

Iterate

Fix, redesign and repeat until the robot — and the team — gets better.

There is no “final” robot. Every match, test and mistake becomes the next version.
