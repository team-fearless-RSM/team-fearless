05 — Quick Answers

Curious about
Team Fearless?

A few starting points for students, educators and people discovering our robotics journey for the first time.

Who is Team Fearless?

A student robotics team at iTeach RSM, Mundhwa, Pune, learning through FTC, NRL and hands-on STEM work.

What is FTC?

FIRST Tech Challenge is a robotics competition where teams design, build, program and operate robots to take on a new challenge each season.

What is NRL for Team Fearless?

2026–27 is our first NRL season. We are currently building and learning; competition results will be shared only when they are available.

Do I need robotics experience to be interested?

No. Curiosity is a good first step. Our website shares the process so students can see the different ways people contribute to robotics.

Can we invite or collaborate with the team?

Use the contact form to tell us about your idea, school session or collaboration request. We will respond based on availability.

Where can I follow updates?

Follow our separate FTC and NRL Instagram pages using the links in the Connect section.
